Recommended Newsletter Structure — Every Issue
📣 Feature Story (1–2)
The main thing families need to know or act on this week. Tied to the event pre-coverage schedule below. ~150–200 words each.
📅 Upcoming Events
Every confirmed event in the next 4 weeks — date, name, brief note. No prose needed. Families scan this every week.
🔧 From the Board + 🔜 On the Horizon
Brief operational updates (volunteer needs, reminders, deadlines). Plus one-liners for TBP items coming into view — keeps families in the loop without over-promising.
